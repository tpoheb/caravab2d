# Загрузочный экран «1000 Дорог»

Создан встроенным инструментом image_gen. Изображение предназначено для фона загрузки; название, прогресс и подсказки накладываются в Unity UI отдельно. Фон подключён к главному меню и экрану загрузки через ApplicationFlow.

## Финальный промпт

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished landscape background artwork for the loading screen of the existing Unity caravan trading game "1000 Дорог".
Primary request: create a polished wide 16:9 game loading-screen illustration, ideally 3840x2160, showing a trading caravan travelling through an Arabian fantasy desert toward an ancient thriving city at golden sunset.
Established art direction: the game's existing card backs and event windows use aged dark wood and parchment textures, finely engraved antique brass / old gold geometric frames, small deep turquoise enamel diamond inlays, and compass-rose ornament. Bring that material language into a restrained thin perimeter frame; keep the scene and caravan dominant.
Scene and subject: an intimate procession of five well-proportioned pack camels and several small robed merchants walking a winding trade road over amber dunes. Rolled rugs, woven panniers and bundled trade goods on the camels convey commerce. In the distance a sand-colored walled city with domes and slender towers, a small oasis of date palms and rugged hazy mountains. Occasional weathered ruins subtly suggest an old civilization, while the city remains inviting and alive.
Style/medium: refined painterly fantasy game key art with confident shapes, textured brushwork, appealing slightly stylized proportions and clear silhouettes. Evoke a richly illustrated tabletop adventure and trading board game; atmospheric and beautiful, not photorealistic and not a cartoon for small children.
Composition/framing: panoramic cinematic landscape, caravan arranged on a sweeping road across the middle-lower part of the picture leading toward the distant city. Spacious sky in the upper center provides a clean readable area for an independently rendered game title. The bottom-center 12 percent should be calm darkened sand / parchment-tone negative space for an independently rendered progress bar and a gameplay tip. Keep important subjects away from the outer edges and these UI-safe zones. Border remains narrow and unobtrusive.
Lighting/mood: amber sunset rim light, dusty luminous atmosphere, inviting adventurous journey, mild mystery, optimism and anticipation. Deep umber shadows, warm ochre and sand highlights, restrained teal accents. Balanced contrast, rich depth, no blown-out sun.
Materials/textures: tactile sand, woven textiles, weathered stone, subtle fine parchment grain and antique brass. A thin engraved border with turquoise corner inlays echoes the game's ornate cards, with no bulky panels.
Constraints: one clean production-ready image, horizontal 16:9, opaque background, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no loading bar or fake interface widgets. No modern objects, no combat, no oversized characters, no excessive glowing magic. Camels have natural anatomy, grounded feet and believable cargo.
